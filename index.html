BANGLADESH FUEL PRICE INTELLIGENCE

How do international fuel markets translate into Bangladesh retail prices?

Bangladesh Fuel Observatory converts grade-specific international refined-product benchmarks and USD/BDT into a Tk/litre market signal, adds an explicitly calibrated domestic layer, and tests the relationship against published Bangladesh price events.

READ THE NUMBERS CORRECTLY

Three different numbers answer three different questions

01

International benchmark equivalent

The selected refined-product benchmark in USD/barrel converted to Bangladeshi taka per litre using the aligned USD/BDT rate.

Market-equivalent value — not a retail price.
02

Model-implied retail estimate

The prior market-window signal plus the domestic calibration estimated from earlier official events.

Independent model output — not an official quote.
03

Official Bangladesh price

The published Bangladesh retail-price event available at or before the model's market-data cutoff.

Observed reference used for comparison.
Bangladesh Fuel Observatory does not define a single “fair price.” It reports the international benchmark equivalent, an independent model-implied retail estimate, and the official Bangladesh price separately so their differences can be examined without mixing concepts.

HOW IT WORKS

Three analytical layers

01

Market transformation

Diesel uses Gasoil 10 ppm, petrol uses RON92, and octane uses RON95. Each USD/barrel benchmark is converted to Tk/litre using the corresponding FX observation.

02

Prior market window

For an official event, the model uses only market observations strictly before the event and within the previous 30 calendar days. Missing benchmark observations remain missing.

03

Domestic calibration

The official price minus the market-window signal is a residual. The primary estimator uses the median of earlier eligible residuals and never calibrates an event with its own same-day official price.

Why 30 days? It is a transparent reproducible approximation to the prior-period / moving-average concept described in the published automatic-pricing framework. It is not presented as a reconstruction of BPC's private accounting or proprietary benchmark feed.

RESEARCH SCOPE

What this project measures and what it does not

Measures

Historical association between public international refined-product observations, USD/BDT and Bangladesh's published retail price events. It evaluates transparent specifications with time-ordered validation and compares them with a naive previous-price reference.

Does not claim

Bangladesh Fuel Observatory does not claim to reproduce BPC's private accounting, define a universally fair price, provide an official forecast, or establish causality. Public benchmark archives are indicative market information rather than licensed commercial assessments.
